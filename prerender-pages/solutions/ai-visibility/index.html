The Real Discoverability Challenge

Being Online Is Not the Same as Being Found

Your website might have useful services yet remain difficult for Google to crawl, thin on buyer-focused answers or inconsistently represented across business directories.

AI-generated answers in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews add another layer where prospective buyers research providers. The practical response is to fix technical gaps, publish accurate information and measure what happens, rather than chase unproven hacks or manufactured rankings.

Unified Search Ecosystem

One Connected Strategy: SEO, GEO and AEO

SEO improves how people and search engines discover your pages. AEO improves how clearly your content answers buyer questions. GEO evaluates and strengthens the public information that AI-powered search experiences may use. The three overlap: accessible pages, original factual content, accurate brand information and responsible measurement form the common foundation.

Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

Technical Foundations & Organic Discovery

We optimise technical foundations, page structure, content and legitimate authority signals for search engines. From local service businesses to Shopify stores and international SaaS sites, we focus on search intent, useful pages and measurable enquiries.

Primary Ecosystem:

Google, Bing, Search Console, Core Web Vitals, Crawl Architecture

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

Brand Citations & AI Answer Presence

We examine how ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI search experiences present information about your business and its competitors. We improve owned facts, helpful explanations and credible third-party references, then document observed mentions and citations over time.

Primary Ecosystem:

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google Gemini, Claude, AI Overviews

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

Conversational Queries & Question-Led Answers

We research the questions customers ask, structure honest answers, update FAQs and help resources, and make content easy to understand. Where applicable we use valid schema markup; there is no universal special markup that guarantees AI answers or Google featured snippets.

Primary Ecosystem:

Direct Answers, Featured Snippets, Voice Phrasing, Valid Schema
