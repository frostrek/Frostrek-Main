The Multi-Channel Challenge

Selling on multiple platforms means managing multiple dashboards — and that's where the chaos begins.

Fragmented Data

Your revenue is spread across multiple platforms — no single source of truth for total sales, inventory, or profit.

Manual Reconciliation

You're downloading CSV exports, copying numbers into spreadsheets, and calculating totals by hand — wasting hours every week.

Blind Spots

A product sells out on one platform while overstocked on another. You don't know until customers complain or capital is tied up in dead stock.

No Predictive Intelligence

You react to demand spikes after they happen. No AI forecasting. No inventory alerts. No market trend signals.

Key Capabilities

Everything you need to run a multi-channel e-commerce business from one screen.

Unified Revenue Dashboard

Real-time consolidation of sales across all your storefronts. See total revenue, orders, and profit in one view — no spreadsheets, no platform hopping.

AI Demand Forecasting

Machine learning models trained on your historical sales data predict demand spikes 7–30 days ahead. Stock the right products before the rush hits.

Cross-Platform Inventory Sync

Live inventory tracking across all storefronts. Auto-alerts when stock falls below thresholds. Prevent overselling and stockouts.

Profit Margin Analysis

Per-product, per-platform profit calculation accounting for platform fees, shipping costs, COGS, and ad spend. Know which products actually make money.

Market Trend Intelligence

AI-powered trend analysis pulls search volume, competitor pricing, and category demand shifts. Spot opportunities before your competitors do.

Automated Reporting

Weekly and monthly performance reports auto-generated and emailed. Investor-ready P&L, SKU performance, channel breakdowns — all automated.
