Delivery Approach

A clear review process from task definition to handoff

We organize the evaluation cycle around calibrated instructions, structured review gates, and traceable audit logs to give engineering teams high-confidence datasets.

Typical Scoped Deliverables:
  • Sample-calibrated evaluation scorecards
  • Ranked preference pairs with rationales (JSONL/Parquet)
  • QA review logs with inter-annotator agreement metrics
  • Source-truth alignment and constraint confirmation
01

Define & Sample

Confirm rubric criteria, select representative prompt and response sets, and define edge-case boundaries.

02

Calibrate Evaluators

Train domain annotators, run blind calibration trials, and align scoring anchors to minimize inter-annotator drift.

03

Primary Labeled Review

Qualified reviewers score and rank candidate outputs against approved rubrics and Source Truth standards.

04

Review & Spot-Check

Senior QA leads arbitrate edge-case disagreements and track inter-annotator agreement (Cohen’s kappa).

05

Delivery & Export

Clean data export with audit records, delivered in client-specified JSONL, Parquet, or CSV schemas.
