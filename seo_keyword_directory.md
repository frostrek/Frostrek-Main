## 1. Executive Summary: The "Long-Tail" Strategy
We have overhauled the codebase to target high-intent, long-tail keywords explicitly focused on key expansion markets (**USA, UK, and India**). By targeting specific, multi-word search queries that actual business buyers use when they want to purchase, we achieve:
*   **Lower Competition:** Easier and faster rankings on search engines.
*   **Higher Conversion Rate:** Traffic coming from these terms consists of serious buyers looking for specific services.
*   **Generative AI Readiness:** Search terms are formatted for conversational queries (e.g., Perplexity, ChatGPT, and Gemini).
